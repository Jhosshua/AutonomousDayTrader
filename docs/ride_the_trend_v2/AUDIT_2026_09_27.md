## Confirmed defects and fixes

1. **Profile rebuild race:** `ProfileStore.build` mutated shared state in an executor.
   Cancelling its asyncio task did not cancel its thread. An old build could overwrite a
   new session's profile; the stale-result cleanup could also delete a new profile.
   Workers now build private values, with publication and error handling guarded by the
   generation on the event loop. The deadline also bounds waiting for the CPU build.
2. **Crossed quotes could pass the book/spread gates:** a cross in the final half-second
   left enough earlier coverage and a recent good quote to return `complete=True`.
   The sole last-cross timestamp also forgot earlier invalid intervals. Crosses are now
   explicit invalid states in quote history and second buckets; an invalid latest state
   cannot pass. Late prints inside an invalid interval remain unclassified after recovery.
3. **Session delta omitted the opening minute:** when the first quote was just after
   09:30:00, ceiling the starting minute discarded all first-minute flow. A regression
   with 10,000 opening buys and 30 later sells previously returned -30; it now returns
   +9,970. Generation totals restart at reconnect and accept only prints at/after that
   exact timestamp, including the partial minute. Midday startup is labeled partial.
4. **Stale profile history accepted:** five old sessions were accepted even when the
   latest five were absent. Input selection now uses the existing verified exchange
   calendar; duplicate minute keys are normalized, and valid early-close sessions use
   their shorter length rather than requiring 300 bars from a 210-minute day.
5. **Wrong CPI date and undefined historical coverage:** November CPI was entered as
   November 12, but BLS lists November 10. The shipped calendar now has explicit verified
   FOMC/CPI/payroll dates and coverage September 1–December 31, 2026, with both date bounds
   enforced. It does not claim to cover every economic release.
   Sources: [BLS CPI schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm),
   [BLS payroll schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/empsit.htm),
   [Federal Reserve meeting calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm).
6. **Missing opening SPY data accepted:** the stock required its 09:30 open but SPY did
   not. SPY now records its first timestamp and the relative-strength gate rejects a
   missing opening bar.
7. **Recovery lock survived subsequent sessions:** `restore_failed` was documented as
   lasting until the next session, but daily reset never cleared it. Daily reset now
   clears that lock along with the discarded session state.
8. **Rejected-print IDs bypassed the memory cap:** ineligible prints occupied ID sets
   without counting toward the raw cap. Their IDs are no longer retained.
9. **Dashboard claims exceeded the rendered UI:** per-stock reasons, macro/wind notes,
   and profile readiness were present in API payloads but not rendered. The card now
   displays them, including plain-language reasons with timestamps, an honest empty
   state, and the entry/add-on switch state. Mode-off and recovery locks also appear
   among card blockers. Tape health now includes observed spread reference/current bps.
10. **Vacuous regression assertion:** an existing recovery assertion ended in `or True`.
    It is replaced by checks for insufficient data and genuine subsequent recovery.

## Remaining boundaries

- Full-depth market-data vendor selection remains open as stated in the brief. No vendor
  was bought or substituted. The live layer remains NBBO and is identified accordingly.
- No historical replay admitted a Ride the Trend order. This audit verifies software
  behavior, not profitability or the occurrence of a future market-session trade.
- Book/spread are sampled at one-second resolution. Trade timestamps and velocity retain
  nanoseconds. Window completeness uses bounded watermark checks (5-second feed slack,
  60-second trade watermark bound; velocity also requires a recent print). They cannot
  prove an upstream stream delivered every print. Late arrivals do not repair all already
  classified successor trades. These source limitations remain explicit.
- Session delta after a restart/outage is a labeled partial total and requires 30 continuous
  minutes. The calendar expires after December 31; unsupported profile calendar years
  fail closed. The volume profile approximates volume from minute-bar price ranges.
- Sunday production health can verify deployment, configuration, durability, matching
  flat paper books, and profile fetching. It cannot verify Monday's scan or fill.

Production verification records the exact pushed commit, Railway deployment status,

keeps unrelated uncommitted research in the original checkout out of this deployment.
