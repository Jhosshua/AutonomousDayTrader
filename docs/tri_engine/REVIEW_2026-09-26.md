# Tri-engine and history follow-up review — 2026-09-26

existing evidence. No test placed orders on the deployed paper account.

## Fixed execution defects

1. **Protection HTTP 429 was a hard rejection.** A mocked protection POST
   reproduced abandonment of the trade. 408/429/5xx remain retryable; 429 honors
   a numeric Retry-After with a five-second minimum. A quote storm cannot bypass
   this delay. Other tranches continue to be managed.
2. **Partial entry risk omitted already-filled shares.** A TSLA partial entry
   reported $186.93 instead of $372.93 of combined filled/pending stop risk.
   Filled shares count at actual cost before tranche allocation, and outstanding
   shares retain their estimate. Both symbols and sides are checked across restart.
3. **One failing poll could block a separate tranche's exit.** A 503 on TSLA's
   first protection group previously prevented the second group's time exit.
   Entry and tranche stages now all get a management attempt before errors are
   reported. Integrity errors take precedence over transient failures.
4. **Protection expired or was canceled while market exits were unavailable.**
   New tri-engine OCOs use GTC. Outside regular hours the controller continues
   polling but retains protection rather than canceling it for an unavailable
   market exit. Restart/next-session recovery is covered. OR15 keeps its existing
   default time in force.
5. **Nonfinal broker states could release a duplicate exit.** `done_for_day`,
   `suspended`, and `stopped` no longer count as final. Each state is tested while
   cancellation is pending; no replacement exit is submitted until settlement.
6. **Late entry shares inherited old fill metadata.** A remainder filled at
   $103 after the original $101 partial fill was recorded at $101. Late shares
   now use their own incremental notional, time, risk and derived levels, and
   still take the immediate reducing-exit path. Recovery preserves that metadata.
7. **Broker errors were absent from the API and card.** Active lifecycle errors
   are now included, displayed, checkpointed on change, and cleared after recovery.
   Short positions say “Buys back.”

Broker mechanics checked against [Alpaca's order documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca):
OCO supports GTC, extended hours are unsupported, and done-for-day orders can
resume in the following session. GTC does not guarantee an overnight fill or
eliminate gaps, corporate actions, broker outages, or both-leg execution races.

The old home page showed only today's empty list after midnight. The history
panel omitted normal daily summaries and placed the September 21 recovered
summary above newer trades. Trades also mixed UTC and Eastern timestamp strings,
putting some 15:55 closes in the wrong order.

- Empty-today dashboard now previews the three latest saved days.
- History shows daily groups newest first, including zero-trade sessions and
  recovered summaries; selecting a day reveals its trades.
- Yesterday is an explicit Eastern-date filter, applied to rows, totals and
  daily summaries. Paging remains available; stale requests cannot overwrite
  a newly selected range and appended rows are deduplicated.
- Ordering and cursor comparisons use actual timestamps, not lexical offset
  strings. A regression covers equal instants represented with different offsets.
- The raw calendar code is translated to “Market closed today,” including in
  pro mode. Other fixed-plan reasons have plain-language explanations.

No historical ledger records were rewritten or reconstructed for these fixes.
