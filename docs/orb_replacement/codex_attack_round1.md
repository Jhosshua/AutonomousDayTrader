Reviewed ADT at `c8194eb` and ORBStraddle at `71b001f`. This was read-only; I did not run tests or contact Alpaca or Railway.

1. **P0 — The external book’s prefix filter misses ordinary bracket exits.** The plan selects orders by `client_order_id` prefix and says that includes bracket legs ([plan:82](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:82)). ORBStraddle puts its prefix on the bracket parent, while its code explicitly says broker-created child legs have UUID client IDs ([orders.py:76](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/orders.py:76), [core.py:3839](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:3839)). After a normal stop or target fill, ADT can keep counting the entry shares after Alpaca is flat; its mismatch check then blocks new entries ([main.py:326](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:326), [main.py:337](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:337), [main.py:434](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:434)). **Fix:** fetch nested prefixed parents and include fills from their child legs by parent relationship, regardless of each child’s client ID; test stop, target, partial fills, and OCO cancellation.

2. **P0 — Today-only signed fills cannot represent a carried position.** The plan uses today’s fills and expects ORBStraddle’s morning sweep to clear prior-day positions ([plan:81](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:81), [plan:88](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:88)). But the sweep can sell a carried own position today ([core.py:2586](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2586), [core.py:2601](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2601)): for a carried long of 10 shares, the today-only external total changes from 0 to −10, while Alpaca changes from +10 to 0. Neither state matches ADT’s local book. **Fix:** reconcile from ORBStraddle’s current signed own quantity, or use a carried-position opening balance plus today’s fills. Add rollover tests for both an uncleared carry and a carry closed by the sweep.

3. **P1 — The independent pre-entry reads leave a same-symbol race, and the TSLA/CDE rationale cites a local-only guard.** ORBStraddle reads account positions and open orders, then rereads before its POST ([core.py:2118](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2118), [core.py:2425](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2425)); ADT’s proposed check has the same check-then-submit shape ([plan:89](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:89)). Both can see a symbol as free before either order appears at Alpaca. Also, ADT tri’s `SYMBOL_ALREADY_COMMITTED` check reads only ADT’s local positions and working orders ([tri_execution.py:339](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/core/tri_execution.py:339)); it cannot see the sidecar on its own. **Fix:** put the external ownership check in the final shared ADT entry-submit path, including tri and OR15, and use an account-wide symbol reservation or other atomic coordination to close the race. Do not rely on tri’s current local check.

4. **P1 — The plan knowingly allows separate bots to exceed one account-level loss limit.** The plan estimates combined losses of about 5.5% ([plan:94](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:94)). ORBStraddle’s account-level loss alarm is explicitly alert-only ([core.py:915](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:915)); its halt measures its own P&L, and its gross exposure is calculated from its own supervision book ([core.py:888](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:888), [core.py:1604](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:1604)). ADT’s breaker liquidates ADT’s local intraday positions ([main.py:1258](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:1258)). These are independent controls, not a hard account-level drawdown cap. **Fix:** define and enforce a combined account risk/drawdown budget, with each service flattening only positions it can prove it owns.

5. **P2 — `ORBS_EXCLUDE_SYMBOLS` needs a precise insertion point or it can waste picks.** Adaptive selection ranks candidates, chooses pick 1, then looks for a second pick in a different sector ([adaptive.py:740](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/adaptive.py:740), [adaptive.py:750](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/adaptive.py:750)). If TSLA/CDE are refused only after that selection, an excluded pick can consume a slot without promoting the next eligible candidate; an `execute()`-only refusal cannot recover candidates it was never given ([core.py:2176](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2176), [core.py:2375](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2375)). Secondary scans dedupe symbols recorded in executions ([scanner.py:1112](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/scanner.py:1112)), so execute-only exclusions can also be reconsidered repeatedly. **Fix:** leave the full board intact for regime scoring, but filter exclusions from the eligible selection pool before choosing pick 1 and pick 2; update the audit rows and keep the execute-time check as a backstop.

6. **P1 — A stale ORB supervision row can mistake an ADT position for an ORB orphan and block the whole sidecar.** ORBStraddle promotes stale supervision by checking whether Alpaca has *any* position in that symbol ([core.py:2842](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2842), [core.py:2851](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2851)). Its preflight refuses to trade when an orphan is recorded ([auditor.py:265](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/auditor.py:265), [auditor.py:441](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/auditor.py:441)). If ADT holds a symbol from a stale ORB supervision row, the sidecar can falsely block all entries. **Fix:** verify stale ownership against ORBStraddle’s own book/order IDs and signed quantity before creating an orphan; test same-symbol external holdings.

7. **P1 — “Stop the service” is unsafe while an ORB position is open.** The rollback permits stopping the service ([plan:123](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:123)). ORBStraddle’s software supervisor manages fast-fail and time-based exits and performs its own 11:00 flatten ([core.py:3162](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:3162), [core.py:3503](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:3503)); stopping the process stops those controls even though broker bracket legs may remain. **Fix:** keep the sidecar running until its own positions and working orders are confirmed flat. If an immediate rollback is needed, explicitly close its own positions and confirm fills before stopping it.

8. **P1 — Require a new, empty, dedicated `/app/state` volume.** The plan specifies a mounted volume but does not say it must be isolated from ORBStraddle’s live volume ([plan:60](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:60)). Autopilot and destination state are stored in `runtime.env` ([auditor.py:40](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/auditor.py:40), [auditor.py:53](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/auditor.py:53)); separately, the scheduler runs the 08:00 morning sweep when order routing is armed, regardless of autopilot status ([app.py:167](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:167), [core.py:2593](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:2593)). Reusing or cloning live state could expose old supervision/book records to a new service that has Alpaca keys. The independent kill switch is named `JSL_KILL`, not `ORBS_*` ([config.py:317](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/config.py:317)). **Fix:** create a fresh sidecar-only volume; verify its book, supervision, orphan file, `runtime.env`, and kill-switch state before arming.

9. **P1 — The documented autopilot POST is not the endpoint’s contract.** The plan says to POST with a fingerprint ([plan:61](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:61)). The handler requires its session token and valid host/origin ([app.py:744](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:744)); it expects a boolean `on` field, computes the destination fingerprint itself, and writes it to `runtime.env` ([app.py:784](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:784), [app.py:805](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:805), [app.py:820](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:820)). **Fix:** arm through the authenticated dashboard flow or send the documented `{"on": true}` request with the active session token; then verify the recorded destination and armed state.

10. **P2 — Same branch does not prove both services run the same build.** The plan says using ORBStraddle `main` means the services “can never drift,” while also acknowledging every push restarts both ([plan:46](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:46)). Its proposed equality check uses `rules_version`, a configuration value, not a source revision ([app.py:669](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/app.py:669), [config.py:148](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/config.py:148)). Separate Railway deployments can fail or roll back independently. **Fix:** expose and compare the deployed commit SHA for both services; promote the same SHA and verify both deployments before arming.

11. **P2 — Equity fallback sizing can fail because this is a shared account.** The primary baseline uses Alpaca `last_equity` when valid ([core.py:1914](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:1914)). If it is unavailable, the paper fallback requires the whole account to have no positions or order/fill activity before the open ([core.py:1963](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:1963), [core.py:1983](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:1983)). ADT overnight holdings can therefore prevent fallback capture and make ORBStraddle refuse to size. **Fix:** verify `last_equity` availability for this account; define an approved baseline path for the shared account or fail the preflight before the first armed morning.

12. **P2 — The “under 200 requests/min” estimate is not a cap.** ORBStraddle’s 100/min budget plus 50/min exit reserve is local to that process ([config.py:298](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/config.py:298)); ADT has no shared limiter, syncs account and positions on its reconcile interval ([config.py:45](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/config.py:45), [main.py:393](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:393)), and polls each linked order every five-second pass ([main.py:390](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/main.py:390), [engine.py:309](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/core/engine.py:309)). Thus “ADT ~25/min” is an estimate, not an enforced ceiling. **Fix:** measure worst-case ADT request load with working orders and add a shared account limiter or reserve enough headroom for both services’ bursts and exits.

13. **P1 — The first armed session is also the first end-to-end check.** The plan arms after the evening checks, then schedules comparison at about 10:20 the next morning ([plan:113](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:113), [plan:117](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/PLAN_2026_09_28_orb_becomes_orbstraddle.md:117)). That comparison comes after the sidecar’s 09:39 decision and possible orders. The listed tests do not explicitly cover bracket-child UUID attribution, carry-opening balances, a same-symbol submit race, or a false orphan from an ADT holding. **Fix:** add those cases to a shared-account integration harness and keep autopilot off for an observation session; arm only after reconciliation and ownership are verified.

**Verdict:** I would not arm this plan as written. ADT’s ordinary purges and flatteners iterate its local orders and positions, and ORBStraddle’s close, sweep, and kill paths are ownership-scoped; the exact `adto-`/`adtoo-` prefixes also do not match ADT’s `adt-` IDs when the hyphen delimiter is enforced ([engine.py:276](/Users/mo/AutonomousDayTrader/backend/app/core/engine.py:276), [config.py:289](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/config.py:289), [core.py:4339](/Users/mo/ORBStraddle/core.py:4339)). Those protections do not solve the two P0 reconciliation errors or the shared-account race and risk-budget gaps. Fix the reconciliation basis and bracket-leg attribution first, then resolve the P1 ownership, arming, and rollback issues before the first armed session.
