## Fixed rules to implement

1. TSLA shares, BUY only, one share, one signal attempt/session. QQQ is context only for this arm. Session minutes are ET with start labels. Valid completed 09:30–09:44 TSLA bars form ORH/ORL/mid. Require contiguous same-session TSLA and QQQ bars with no premarket contamination, duplicates, reversal of time, stale data, or carried-forward QQQ.
2. Frozen nonstandard ATR: `max(high-low, abs(high-previous_close))`, rolling simple mean of the last 14; >=3 observations, otherwise 0.005*close. Use the current completed signal bar, never later data.
3. Scan starts 09:45–11:30 inclusive. First strict close above ORH arms; only a later bar can signal. Retest low <= ORH+0.20*ATR and >=mid, close>open and close>=ORH; exact-minute QQQ close>=cumulative typical-price volume-weighted regular-session VWAP. Consume the first qualified signal even if risk, data or broker rejects it.
4. Decision after T closes at T+1. Stage until T+2, then attempt one market order using fresh price evidence and positive risk. Do not submit at T+1 or wait for the T+2 completed bar in production. Offline replay uses exactly the T+2 raw open. Expire missed entry windows and never catch up a stale entry on restart. Reject after session close minus 30 minutes.
5. Fixed initial stop ORL; target actual entry+2*(entry-ORL), single full exit. No VIX/EMA/SPY/RVOL/news/ORB arbitration changes. Account loss halt, persistence health, buying power, symbol exclusion and existing account caps remain enforced and logged as account-level skips. Never alter ORL to pass generic stop-width bounds.
6. Manage from fill: stop, target, 120 minutes, or official close minus 5 minutes, whichever occurs first. Offline model uses stop-first ambiguous bars, gap-down open stop price, touch target at exact target, and terminal time open before terminal high/low. Production uses actual executable broker fills; protective stop and forced-flat procedure must be tested, including feed disconnection and restart. Record deviations, never invent exit prices.
7. Persist the complete session state, sole-signal latch, pending order/times, entry/fill/exit state, skip reason, all evaluated retests, actual fills, fees/spread/slippage, cost assumptions (3 and 6 bps per side), and source/code hashes.
